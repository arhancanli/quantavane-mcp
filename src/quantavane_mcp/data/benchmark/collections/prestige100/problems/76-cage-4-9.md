# 76. The (4,9)-cage

Family: Cages. Benchmark problem: `cage-4-9`.

**Best known:** n(4,9) ≤ 270, G. Exoo, J. Goedgebeur, J. Jooken, T. Stubbe and G. Van den Eede (2026). Also known: n(4,9) ≥ 161 (the Moore bound).

**To beat it:** a 4-regular graph of girth at least 9 on fewer than 270 vertices.

## The problem

Find a 4-regular simple graph whose girth (shortest cycle) is at least 9, with as few vertices n as possible; it shows n(4,9) <= n (cages grow with the girth). The best known (k,g)-graph in the House of Graphs cage table has 270 vertices [EGJSV26]; a graph with fewer vertices improves it.

## Why it matters

The 4-regular cage of girth 9 is open between the Moore bound 161 and 270; the 2026 graph of Exoo, Goedgebeur, Jooken, Stubbe and Van den Eede beat Exoo's 275 from about 2008. Girth 9 is where the cage problem stops being settled for degree 4 (n(4,7) = 67 and n(4,8) = 80 are known).

Sources: [House of Graphs, cage table](https://houseofgraphs.org/meta-directory/cages); [Exoo and Jajcay, Dynamic Cage Survey, Electron. J. Combin. DS16 (2013)](https://doi.org/10.37236/37).
