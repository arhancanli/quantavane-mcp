## The problem

Find a strongly regular graph with parameters (88, 27, 6, 9): 88 vertices, 27-regular, adjacent vertices with exactly 6 common neighbours and non-adjacent vertices with exactly 9. It is the smallest parameter set marked open in A. E. Brouwer's tables. A valid graph scores 1.

## Why it matters

On 9 Nov 2026 srg(88, 27, 6, 9) is the smallest parameter set marked open in Brouwer's tables of strongly regular graphs: every set on fewer vertices is marked decided, several of them recently. Deciding the smallest open case is a natural test for constructive and computer-assisted methods.

Sources: [A. E. Brouwer, strongly regular graphs on 1-50 and 51-100 vertices](https://www.win.tue.nl/~aeb/graphs/srg/srgtab.html).
