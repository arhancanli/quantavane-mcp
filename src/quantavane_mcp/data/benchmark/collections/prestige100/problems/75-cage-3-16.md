# 75. The (3,16)-cage

Family: Cages. Benchmark problem: `cage-3-16`.

**Best known:** n(3,16) ≤ 702, [P26] in the House of Graphs table. Also known: n(3,16) ≥ 512 (the Dynamic Cage Survey (2013)).

**To beat it:** a 3-regular graph of girth at least 16 on fewer than 702 vertices.

## The problem

Find a 3-regular simple graph whose girth (shortest cycle) is at least 16, with as few vertices n as possible; it shows n(3,16) <= n (cages grow with the girth). The best known (k,g)-graph in the House of Graphs cage table has 702 vertices [P26]; a graph with fewer vertices improves it.

## Why it matters

The cubic cage of girth 16 has between 512 and 702 vertices; the upper bound dropped from 960 to 936 and then to 702 in 2026. Such large jumps show how far the known graphs may still be from the true cages.

Sources: [House of Graphs, cage table](https://houseofgraphs.org/meta-directory/cages); [Exoo and Jajcay, Dynamic Cage Survey, Electron. J. Combin. DS16 (2013)](https://doi.org/10.37236/37).
