# 66. Conway's 99-graph, srg(99, 14, 1, 2)

Family: Moore graph and strongly regular graphs. Benchmark problem: `srg-99-14-1-2`.

**Best known:** none known, none.

**To beat it:** a graph on 99 vertices in which every edge lies in exactly one triangle and every two non-adjacent vertices have exactly two common neighbours: srg(99, 14, 1, 2).

**Keeper:** [Parameters of strongly regular graphs (A. E. Brouwer)](https://www.win.tue.nl/~aeb/graphs/srg/srgtab.html), read 2026-11-09 15:52 +04 (tables for 1-50 and 51-100 vertices).

## The problem

Find a graph on 99 vertices, 14-regular, in which every two adjacent vertices have exactly one common neighbour and every two non-adjacent vertices exactly two: a strongly regular graph srg(99, 14, 1, 2). J. H. Conway offered $1,000 for deciding whether it exists. A valid graph scores 1.

## Why it matters

John Conway offered $1,000 for deciding whether this graph exists, one of his five $1,000 problems (2014). Brouwer's table of strongly regular graphs still marks srg(99, 14, 1, 2) as unknown (read 9 Nov 2026).

Sources: [Conway, Five $1000 Problems (2014), linked from OEIS A248380](https://oeis.org/A248380); [A. E. Brouwer, strongly regular graphs on 51-100 vertices](https://www.win.tue.nl/~aeb/graphs/srg/srgtab51-100.html).
