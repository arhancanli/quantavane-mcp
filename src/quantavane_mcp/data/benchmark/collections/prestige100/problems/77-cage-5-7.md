## The problem

Find a 5-regular simple graph whose girth (shortest cycle) is at least 7, with as few vertices n as possible; it shows n(5,7) <= n (cages grow with the girth). The best known (k,g)-graph in the House of Graphs cage table has 152 vertices [MY]; a graph with fewer vertices improves it.

## Why it matters

The 5-regular cage of girth 7 has between 106 (the Moore bound) and 152 vertices. It is the smallest open cage of degree 5, since n(5,5) = 30 and n(5,6) = 42 are known.

Sources: [House of Graphs, cage table](https://houseofgraphs.org/meta-directory/cages); [Exoo and Jajcay, Dynamic Cage Survey, Electron. J. Combin. DS16 (2013)](https://doi.org/10.37236/37).
