# 73. The (3,14)-cage

Family: Cages. Benchmark problem: `cage-3-14`.

**Best known:** n(3,14) ≤ 372, [EK26] in the House of Graphs table. Also known: n(3,14) ≥ 258 (B. D. McKay, W. Myrvold and J. Nadon (1998)).

**To beat it:** a 3-regular graph of girth at least 14 on fewer than 372 vertices.

## The problem

Find a 3-regular simple graph whose girth (shortest cycle) is at least 14, with as few vertices n as possible; it shows n(3,14) <= n (cages grow with the girth). The best known (k,g)-graph in the House of Graphs cage table has 372 vertices [EK26]; a graph with fewer vertices improves it.

## Why it matters

The cubic cage of girth 14 has between 258 and 372 vertices; the upper bound fell from Exoo's 384 to 372 in 2026 (House of Graphs). Cubic cages of girth 13 to 16 are the smallest open cases of the cage problem.

Sources: [House of Graphs, cage table](https://houseofgraphs.org/meta-directory/cages); [Exoo and Jajcay, Dynamic Cage Survey, Electron. J. Combin. DS16 (2013)](https://doi.org/10.37236/37).
