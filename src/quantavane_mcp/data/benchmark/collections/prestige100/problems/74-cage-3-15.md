# 74. The (3,15)-cage

Family: Cages. Benchmark problem: `cage-3-15`.

**Best known:** n(3,15) ≤ 620, N. Biggs and M. J. Hoare (1983). Also known: n(3,15) ≥ 384 (the Dynamic Cage Survey (2013)).

**To beat it:** a 3-regular graph of girth at least 15 on fewer than 620 vertices.

## The problem

Find a 3-regular simple graph whose girth (shortest cycle) is at least 15, with as few vertices n as possible; it shows n(3,15) <= n (cages grow with the girth). The best known (k,g)-graph in the House of Graphs cage table has 620 vertices [BH83]; a graph with fewer vertices improves it.

## Why it matters

The cubic cage of girth 15 has between 384 and 620 vertices; Biggs and Hoare's 620-vertex graph from 1983 is still the smallest known. The 43-year-old bound makes it one of the most stable entries of the cage table.

Sources: [House of Graphs, cage table](https://houseofgraphs.org/meta-directory/cages); [Exoo and Jajcay, Dynamic Cage Survey, Electron. J. Combin. DS16 (2013)](https://doi.org/10.37236/37).
